New, open box, used and refurbished: what each means

  • New: Check that the seller is an established retailer with the original manufacturer warranty, and that the listing is not a gray-market import.
  • Open box: Open box means returned or opened, usually in working order. Ask whether the original box, accessories and warranty are included.
  • Refurbished: Look at who refurbished it: manufacturer or eBay-certified programs carry a warranty, seller-refurbished items often do not.

Checklist before you buy a used RTX 5060 gaming laptop

  1. Check the seller: feedback score, account history and a return policy you can actually use.
  2. Ask for photos of the actual laptop, including the serial number sticker. Stock photos alone are a warning sign.
  3. Check the exact CPU, RAM, storage and screen (resolution and refresh rate) in the listing. Two laptops with the same GPU can be very different machines.
  4. Ask about hinge play, dead pixels, keyboard wear and whether the fans are noisy under load.
  5. Ask whether the manufacturer warranty transfers to a new owner. This differs by brand, so confirm with the seller.
  6. Laptop GPUs with the same name differ a lot by power limit (TGP). Check the wattage in the specs, not just the GPU name, before comparing prices.
  7. Ask for a photo of the battery health report and check the charger is included and original.
  8. Test it as soon as it arrives, during the return window. Run a stress test and watch temperatures.
  9. Be careful with prices far below the median in the table above, and with listings that say box only, for parts or untested.

How much does a new RTX 5060 gaming laptop cost on eBay?

Is refurbished better than used?
