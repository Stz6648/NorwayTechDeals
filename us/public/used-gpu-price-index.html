Methodology

We collect fixed-price eBay US listings for each model several times a day through the eBay API. For every model and day we take the median asking price of working units in used condition (and separately, new), after removing parts-only, untested, bundle and box-only listings and anything below a sanity floor for that model. A model needs at least 5 listings on a day to count. The headline index is the median across models of each model's used median relative to November 3, 2026 (= 100), so one expensive card cannot dominate it.

These are asking prices, not completed sales, and listings come and go, so small day-to-day moves are noise. Treat changes under about 2% as flat.
