How to buy a used graphics card safely

  • Ask for or look for photos that show the card and its serial number, not stock images.
  • Prefer listings that describe how the card was tested, and that accept returns.
  • Check the seller's feedback score and recent negative reviews.
  • Avoid listings marked for parts, untested, or sold as-is.
  • Compare the total price including shipping and sales tax, not just the item price.
  • Check that your power supply and case suit the card before you buy.
