Checklist before you buy a used RTX 3060

  1. Check the seller: feedback score, account history and a return policy you can actually use.
  2. Ask for photos of the actual card, including the serial number sticker. Stock photos alone are a warning sign.
  3. Ask whether it was used for mining, rendering or in a server. Heavy long-term use wears fans and thermal pads faster than light gaming.
  4. Ask whether the manufacturer warranty transfers to a new owner. This differs by brand, so confirm with the seller.
  5. Test it as soon as it arrives, during the return window. Run a stress test and watch temperatures.
  6. Be careful with prices far below the median in the table above, and with listings that say box only, for parts or untested.

RTX 3060 price FAQ

Is refurbished better than used?

It depends on who refurbished it. Manufacturer or eBay-certified programs usually carry a warranty. A seller's own refurbishment often does not, so read the listing before you buy.

How often is this page updated?

The catalogue is refreshed from eBay automatically and the date at the top of the page shows the latest snapshot. The price on eBay is always the one that counts.

Before you buy

Always compare the exact variant, check the seller's feedback and return policy, and read the full listing. A low price is only a good deal if the item is what it says it is.
