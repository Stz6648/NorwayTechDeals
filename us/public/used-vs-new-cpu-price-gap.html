What to check before buying a used CPU

  • Look for photos of the actual processor, including the pins or contact pads, not stock images.
  • Make sure the listing is the exact model you need and not a similar name (for example X3D vs non-X3D, K vs KF).
  • Check your motherboard socket and BIOS support before you buy. A CPU that fits the socket may still need a BIOS update.
  • Prefer sellers with a clear return policy and high feedback, and keep the listing screenshot until the CPU is installed and tested.
  • A CPU sold without a cooler is normal for K-series and X3D parts. Budget for a cooler if you do not have one.

Is used or refurbished worth it for a CPU?

For many buyers the answer is yes, if the discount is meaningful and the seller accepts returns. If the used price is only a few dollars below new, buying new with the manufacturer warranty is usually the better choice.

Prices change quickly. The price you see on eBay is the one that counts.
