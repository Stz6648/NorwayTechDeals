New, open box, used and refurbished: what each means

  • New: Check that the seller is an established retailer with the original manufacturer warranty, and that the listing is not a gray-market import.
  • Open box: Open box means returned or opened, usually in working order. Ask whether the original box, accessories and warranty are included.
  • Used: Prefer sellers with high feedback and a clear return policy. Ask for photos of the actual card and, for GPUs, whether it was used for mining.

Checklist before you buy a used Ryzen 7 9700X

  1. Check the seller: feedback score, account history and a return policy you can actually use.
  2. Ask for photos of the actual processor, including the serial number sticker. Stock photos alone are a warning sign.
  3. Look for bent pins or marks on the socket side, and ask whether the processor was overclocked.
  4. Ask whether the manufacturer warranty transfers to a new owner. This differs by brand, so confirm with the seller.
  5. Test it as soon as it arrives, during the return window. Run a stress test and watch temperatures.
  6. Be careful with prices far below the median in the table above, and with listings that say box only, for parts or untested.
