About Used Tech Radar

Used Tech Radar is an independent site that compares prices for new, open-box, used and refurbished PC hardware on eBay. We earn a commission when you buy through our links, which is how the site is funded.

We do not sell anything ourselves and we have not tested the products listed. Our prices come from a catalogue snapshot and can be out of date by the time you read them.
