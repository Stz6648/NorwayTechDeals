What the eBay refurbished grades mean

According to eBay's refurbished program pages, items sold under the program are tested and fully functional. Certified Refurbished items come in like-new condition from the brand or an authorized seller and carry a two-year warranty. Excellent, Very Good and Good items come from vetted sellers, differ in how much cosmetic wear they show, and carry a one-year warranty.

Seller Refurbished is different. It is a condition an individual seller chooses, it is not part of the official program, and the program's warranty terms are not stated to apply to it. Read that listing carefully.

eBay also describes a 30-day return window for program items and covers purchases with its Money Back Guarantee. The warranty has exclusions, including purchases made outside the US and accidental or cosmetic damage. Terms can change, so confirm them on the listing and on eBay's program pages before you buy.
