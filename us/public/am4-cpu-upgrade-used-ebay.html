How we chose

We show the lowest-priced qualifying listing in each condition from our latest catalogue snapshot, after skipping listings that look like parts, lots or bundles. The cheapest listing is not always the best deal, so read the checklist.

Check these before you upgrade

  • Your motherboard model and chipset: look up the manufacturer's CPU support list for the exact CPU.
  • BIOS version: some older boards need a BIOS update before a newer CPU will boot, which can be hard to do without a working CPU.
  • Cooling: the 5800X3D runs hot and does not come with a cooler, so check that your cooler is good enough.
  • Whether you actually need it: if your graphics card is the limit in your games, a new CPU will not help much.

Used or new?

CPUs have no moving parts, so a used one in good condition usually works as well as a new one. Prefer sellers who accept returns and show photos of the actual processor, including the pins or contacts.
